### 5. AI follow-up questions

Clients often provide incomplete answers when filling out the questionnaire.

Would it be possible to add a step where, after they submit their responses, the AI reviews the information and asks any necessary follow-up questions before generating the estimate?


## AI automation input at the start?

For the last note, **"AI automation input at start.?????"**, if you're asking what should happen before the estimate process begins, you could phrase it like this:

> **AI Automation at the Start**
>
> Before generating an estimate, the AI should first collect the necessary project information through an interactive questionnaire. If the submitted information is insufficient or unclear, the AI should automatically ask follow-up questions until it has enough details to generate an accurate estimate. Only then should it proceed with the quotation.

This clearly defines the intended AI workflow for the developer.
