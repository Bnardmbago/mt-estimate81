# Questionnaire Form - data fields

| # | Field | What to enter | Required |
|---|--------|---------------|----------|
| 1 | Project name | Short name or working title for this estimate or project | Yes |
| 2 | Nature of work | Whether this is a new build, major enhancement to an existing system, replacement, migration, or a mix | Yes |
| 3 | Scope boundaries | What is clearly **in scope** and what is **out of scope** or deferred | Yes |
| 4 | Project overview | What the project is for, the problem it solves, and what “done” looks like | Yes |
| 5 | Type of system | What you are building (for example: website, mobile app, enterprise system, desktop software, mainly APIs) | Yes |
| 6 | Business domain | The industry or area the system serves (for example: finance, healthcare, government, retail) | Yes |
| 7 | Main functional needs | What the system should do for users and the business, in your own words | Yes |
| 8 | Non-functional needs | Performance, security, availability, expected load, and similar constraints | Yes |
| 9 | Users and load | How many people will use it, busy periods, and how you expect usage to grow | Yes |
| 10 | Connections to other systems | Other systems or data sources this must work with | Yes |
| 11 | Data complexity | How complex the data is (for example: simple, moderate, or complex) | Yes |
| 12 | User interface complexity | How rich or demanding the screens and workflows are (for example: low, medium, or high) | Yes |
| 13 | Technology preferences | Languages, platforms, or tools you want or must use | No |
| 14 | Development approach | How you want the work run (for example: agile, waterfall, or mixed) | Yes |
| 15 | Rules and standards to follow | Laws, standards, or organizational rules the system must meet | Yes |
| 16 | Team and resources | How many people you have, skills you need, or limits on who does the work | Yes |
| 17 | Where development happens | Mainly in Japan, mainly offshore, or a mix | Yes |
| 18 | Delivery timing | When you need delivery, key dates, or fixed windows | Yes |
| 19 | Maintenance and support | What you expect after go-live (support, who operates it, warranty, and similar) | Yes |
| 20 | Risks and unknowns | Things that might change the estimate: dependencies, open questions, or assumptions | Yes |
| 21 | Budget | Rough budget or range in yen | No |
